This Hybrid OpenType font complying with the ISO 15919 standard (previously
URW Palladio UNI) includes precomposed Latin characters (via OpenType GS
using the ccmp feature for precomposed characters in decomposed forms), two
Latin characters and metrics symbols that are allocated to codepoints in the
Private Use Area by the font's author. Many precomposed Latin characters in
this font are also supported by IndUni (allocated to different codepoints in
the Private Use Area). To request this font to their friends, please send an
email to the author from the mapping file's contact info or visit
http://www.sanskritweb.net/imprint.htm (page in English and German)
