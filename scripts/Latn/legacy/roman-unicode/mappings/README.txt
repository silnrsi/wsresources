This font includes precomposed Latin, Greek and Cyrillic characters that are
allocated to reserved codepoints in the Latin, Greek and Cyrillic ranges of
Unicode and codepoints in the Private Use Area by the font's author. The
U+E500-U+E6FC range was under construction as mappings were most likely to be
changed according to Every Witch Way's font page. Note that this font does
not have combining diacritics.

The Roman Unicode font contains scripts that Times New Roman does not have,
and vice versa (This font supports Bengali, Bopomofo, Chrysanthi Runes,
Devanagari, Dlatci, Gothic, Hiragana, Katakana and Tibetan while Times New
Roman supports Arabic, Armenian, Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew and Latin).

Although many Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and Hebrew characters in this font are
also supported by versions of Times New Roman that ships with Windows Vista
and higher, most glyphs do not have the same metrics.
